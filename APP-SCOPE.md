# JMUCC App

Keeps everyone informed during the event through announcements and a synced schedule, and helps participants find answers in the rulebook on demand.

There is nothing to download from an app store. The app installs straight from a web link to the home screen of any phone.

---

## Installing the App

Anyone attending opens the a link in their phone's browser - likely will be emailed to them. Press a few buttons, it adds the app to their home screen. Once added, it behaves like a normal app — its own icon, full screen, and push notifications.

---

## First Open

Everyone gets a personal PIN ahead of time. Enter it once and you're in — no separate password, no picking your name from a list.

OC members each have their own PIN too, which unlocks their special tools — there's no separate shared admin code anymore.

---

## What Each User Can Do

### Delegate / Volunteer / Coach (Participants)
- **Receive announcements** from the OC as push notifications throughout the event.
- **See a schedule** on the homepage, synced from Google Calendar — shows the events relevant to their role and team.
- **Search the rulebook** by typing a question in plain English. It's not a chatbot — no back-and-forth conversation — it finds and highlights the exact relevant quotes from the official rulebook PDFs, so you see the source material directly instead of an AI-written answer.

Coaches are tied to their team; otherwise their experience is the same as delegates. Some volunteers ("team ambassadors") are also tagged to a specific team so they see that team's schedule too.

### Organizing Committee (OC)

Each OC member has their own PIN that unlocks the admin area. From there they run the event:

- **Send announcements** to any group — by role, by division, by team letter, or any combination (e.g. "all coaches in Division 3").
  - **Quiet option:** the message arrives silently — no sound or buzz — and just appears in the app and notification tray.
- **See who's engaged** — for any announcement, a high-level view like "19/40 volunteers, 22/24 schools have opened the app since this was sent," with the option to drill into named specifics.

---

## Overall

1. **Before the event:** organizers load the roster (with everyone's PIN), the school list, the rulebook PDFs, and the team/role calendars.
2. **As people arrive:** each person installs the app and enters their PIN.
3. **At the opening ceremony:** the OC publishes the division and team assignments.
4. **Throughout the week:** the OC sends targeted announcements and tracks engagement; participants stay informed, see the schedule, and get their questions answered.
