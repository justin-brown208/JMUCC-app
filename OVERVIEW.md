# JMUCC App — What It Does

A plain-language tour of the app: every screen, what you can do on it, and what
you can learn from it. No technical detail — just the experience from a
participant's side of the glass.

The app is a **web app you install to your phone's home screen** — no app store,
no download wait. It's built for one week: the JMUCC case competition, used by
everyone on site (delegates, coaches, organizers, and volunteers).

---

## Getting in

Everyone is given a personal **6-digit PIN** before the event. You open the app,
type your PIN, and you're in — that's the entire sign-up. The app already knows
your name, your role, and (if you're on a team) which team you're on, so there's
nothing else to fill out.

- **You stay signed in.** Enter the PIN once; the app remembers you until you
  deliberately log out. No re-typing it every morning.
- **It's personal to you.** Your PIN *is* your identity in the app — the schedule,
  messages, and help options you see are all tailored to who you are.
- **Installing it:** on iPhone you add it to your home screen from Safari's Share
  menu; on Android the browser offers to install it for you. Once installed it
  behaves like any other app, including notifications.

## Home — your hub

After signing in you land on Home, which pulls together the things you care about
most and links out to everything else.

**What's on it:**

- **A greeting** in the corner ("Hello ___") — tap it to log out.
- **The schedule widget.** The current or next-up event is shown large, with
  later events listed smaller beneath it. This *is* the calendar — there's no
  separate calendar page to dig into. It automatically shows the events that apply
  to you (see *Schedule* below).
- **The latest announcement**, shown in full, so the most recent word from the
  organizers is always right there.
- **Your help buttons** (if your role can use them) — see *Asking for help*.
- **Links** to Previous Messages, Competition Rules, and — if you're a queue
  worker or an organizer — to your queue or the admin area.

What you see here shifts based on your role. A delegate, a runner, and an
organizer all open Home to a slightly different set of buttons — only what's
relevant to them.

## The schedule

The schedule is built from the official event calendars, and you only ever see
the parts that apply to **you** — automatically, with no setup.

- You see the calendar for **your role** (for example, a volunteer sees the
  volunteer schedule).
- If you're on a **team** (delegates, coaches, and team ambassadors), you also see
  **your team's** schedule merged in.
- The soonest event is always featured at the top so you can tell at a glance
  what's happening now and what's next.
- A **View Full Week** button opens the whole week's schedule as a single
  **zoomable image** — pinch or scroll to zoom in on any day or session. Handy
  when you want the big picture instead of just what's next.

You don't pick or configure any of this — the right events simply appear.

## Announcements

The organizers send **push notifications** during the event — reminders, updates,
and urgent notices.

- The **most recent** message is always shown on Home.
- **Previous Messages** gives you the full history of everything you've been sent,
  newest first, each shown in full (messages are short, so there's nothing to
  expand or tap into).
- Notifications can be sent **quietly** — they still arrive and appear in the app,
  just without a sound or buzz, so late-night logistics notes don't wake anyone.
- You only receive messages meant for you. Organizers target messages by role and
  team, so a "Team A delegates" note never clutters everyone else's feed.

## Competition Rules — ask a question

Instead of scrolling a PDF hunting for a rule, you can **ask a question in plain
English** and get the exact relevant passages back.

- Type something like *"Can we bring printed notes into the room?"* and submit.
- The app searches the official **rulebook** and **FAQ** and returns the
  **actual quoted passages** that answer you — the real text from the documents,
  with the relevant wording highlighted, and the most relevant results first.
- It's **not a chatbot.** It doesn't chat, guess, or paraphrase — it only shows
  you real, quoted text from the official documents, so you can trust what you
  read. There's no back-and-forth; each question is its own quick lookup.
- If nothing in the documents matches, it tells you plainly rather than making
  something up.
- You can also open the **full rulebook** or **full FAQ** as a normal PDF to read
  end to end.

## Asking for help (requests)

During the event you can raise a quick "please help me" request and be handled in
turn, instead of chasing someone down. There are three help lines:

- **Ask a Rules Question** — routed to the VP Academics (rules and judging
  questions).
- **Request a Runner** — for logistics and errands.
- **Report a Tech Issue** — for A/V and technical problems.

**Who can use which:**

- **Coaches** can ask **rules questions**.
- **Volunteers** can use **any** of the three lines.
- Delegates and organizers don't raise requests through here.

You only ever see the buttons for the lines you're allowed to use.

**Raising a request:**

- You tap the button for the kind of help you need — the request already knows
  which line it belongs to.
- You can add a short note (or leave it blank — many are simply "please call me"),
  a room number if relevant, and a phone number. Your **phone number is remembered
  on your device** so you only type it once, ever.
- Your name is filled in automatically.

**After you submit — the queue:**

This is the part that keeps you from waiting in the dark. Once your request is in,
you see it on Home with your **live position in line** — "You're #3" — and it
**counts down on its own** as the people ahead of you are helped. You never have
to ask "am I next yet?"

- You get a notification when **someone picks up** your request ("someone's on
  it") and again when it's **resolved**.
- You can **cancel** your own request any time if you sorted it out yourself.
- Your position only ever moves **forward** — it never sends you to the back.

You only see **your own** requests and your place in line — never anyone else's
details.

---

## For queue workers (volunteers and the VP Academics)

Some people don't just raise requests — they **work** a help line:

- **Tech Volunteers** work the tech line.
- **Runners** work the runner line.
- The **VP Academics** works the rules line.

If that's you, Home gives you a **My Queue** view of your line:

- The **waiting requests in order**, oldest first — each showing who needs help,
  their room, their note (or "Call me"), their phone number, and when they asked.
- You **claim** a request to take it (once you've claimed it, it's yours — no one
  else can grab the same one), then mark it **resolved** when you're done. Both
  actions notify the person who asked.
- If you can't take one after all, you can **drop** it — it quietly goes back to
  the waiting list for another worker to pick up. The person who asked isn't
  notified and keeps their place in line.
- You can handle requests **out of order** when you need to — the waiting people
  still see a fair, sensible position based on when they asked.

## For the Organizing Committee (admins)

Organizers with admin access get an **Admin area**, reached from Home, for running
communications during the event.

**Compose a Notification** — send a targeted message:

- **Pick a title** from a short preset list (Message / Announcement /
  Reminder) — quick and consistent.
- **Write the message**, and choose whether to send it **quietly** (no sound or
  buzz).
- **Choose who gets it:** start by picking one or more **roles** (with an
  "All Volunteers" shortcut), then optionally **narrow** it down — to certain
  divisions, certain team letters, or a single named school.
- Before it sends, you get a **plain-language confirmation** of exactly who will
  receive it ("This goes to all Coaches in Division 3, Team A"), so there are no
  surprises.

**Message Tracking** — see how a message landed:

- For any message you've sent, see **how many people have opened the app since**
  you sent it — broken down by group ("19 of 40 volunteers, 22 of 24 schools").
- You can **drill in** to see the named list for any group, so you know who to
  nudge in person.

## What different people see, at a glance

| You are… | You get… |
|---|---|
| **Delegate** | Schedule (your role + your team), announcements, rules search |
| **Coach** | The above, plus you can **ask rules questions** |
| **Team Ambassador** | Schedule (your role + your assigned team), announcements, rules search |
| **Volunteer** (Tech, Runner, Room Coordinator, General, Ambassador) | The above, plus you can raise **any** kind of request — and if you're a Tech Volunteer or Runner, you **work** your queue |
| **VP Academics** | Everything a volunteer sees, plus you **work the rules-question queue** |
| **Organizer** | Schedule, announcements, rules search |
| **Organizer with admin access** | The above, plus the **Admin area** (compose + track messages) |

Everyone, whatever their role, gets: **their schedule, their announcements, plain-
English rules search, and one-tap install with notifications.** Everything is
personalized automatically from your PIN — nobody has to set anything up, and you
only ever see what's meant for you.
